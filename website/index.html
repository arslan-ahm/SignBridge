Under The Hood

How it works

📷

See

Your webcam frame is analyzed live, entirely on your device.

→
✋

Track

MediaPipe finds 21 points on your hand — no video ever leaves your browser.

→
🤖

Recognize

A trained Random Forest model turns those points into a letter, A–Z.

→
✍️

Compose

An LLM — running on a real Render Workflow — turns letters into a real sentence.

→
🔊

Speak

Your browser reads the sentence out loud.
